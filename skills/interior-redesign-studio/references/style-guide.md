# Style Cheat-Sheet — Signifiers for Prompts

For each style, fold 2–3 of these concrete signifiers into the prompt. Vague style names
("modern") render poorly; specifics ("low-profile walnut, boucle, paper-shade lamps") render well.

> **Scope:** use these only for the items inside the **CHANGE** list (or in full restyle mode).
> In faithful mode, built elements and unapproved finishes stay as modeled — do not apply a style's
> signifiers to them.

## Scandinavian
Light oak/ash wood, white & soft grey walls, wool throws, simple clean-lined furniture,
green plants, abundant natural light, cozy minimal (hygge). Palette: white, pale wood, soft grey, sage.

## Japandi (Japanese + Scandinavian)
Low-profile furniture, oak + black accents, muted clay/off-white/charcoal, paper-shade
lighting, handmade ceramics, wabi-sabi imperfection, decluttered. Palette: clay, off-white, charcoal, oak.

## Mid-Century Modern
Walnut, tapered legs, organic curves, mustard/teal/burnt-orange accents, statement
pendant, low sideboard, geometric rug. Palette: walnut, cream, mustard, teal.

## Minimalist
Monochrome, hidden storage, few but sculptural pieces, matte finishes, negative space,
no clutter. Palette: white, greige, black, one muted accent.

## Industrial
Exposed brick, black metal, concrete, leather, Edison bulbs, reclaimed wood, open shelving.
Palette: charcoal, rust, tan leather, raw concrete.

## Contemporary Luxe
Marble, brass/gold accents, velvet upholstery, large statement lighting, layered neutrals
with a jewel accent, plush textures. Palette: cream, taupe, brass, deep emerald or navy.

## Boho / Eclectic
Layered textiles, rattan, macramé, plants, warm earthy tones, vintage mix, patterned rugs.
Palette: terracotta, ochre, cream, olive.

## Coastal
Light woods, white + blue palette, linen, woven textures, airy and bright, natural fibers.
Palette: white, soft blue, sand, driftwood.

## Modern Farmhouse
Shiplap, warm woods, black hardware, neutral palette, cozy textiles, apron sink (kitchens).
Palette: warm white, oak, black, sage.

## Art Deco
Bold geometry, brass, lacquer, deep jewel tones, velvet, fluted detailing, statement mirrors.
Palette: emerald, navy, gold, black.

## Lighting layers to mention (any style)
- Ambient: overall daylight or ceiling fixture.
- Task: reading lamp, under-cabinet, pendant over surface.
- Accent: wall sconce, picture light, LED strip — warm 2700K for evening mood.

## Common room-type anchors
- Living: sofa + rug + coffee table + lighting + art.
- Bedroom: bed + headboard + nightstands + soft layered lighting + textiles.
- Kitchen: cabinet fronts + counter material + backsplash + hardware + pendant lights.
- Home office: desk + ergonomic chair + shelving + task light.
- Dining: table + chairs + statement pendant + sideboard.
